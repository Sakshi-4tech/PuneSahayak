# Existing Solutions and PuneSahayak's Scope

Reviewed 15 November 2026. This is a feature comparison, not a claim that PuneSahayak is superior to the listed services.

| Existing solution | What it does well | Scope not provided by PuneSahayak / limitation in comparison | PuneSahayak's focused difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | Multi-mode directions, route alternatives and estimated times; can use traffic and transit schedules where available. | It is a broad navigation product. PuneSahayak is not a replacement and does not provide equivalent live navigation, map coverage or operational data. | A student-oriented view of estimated monthly commute affordability, local expense logging and a transparent cost/time score alongside a dataset-based PMPML journey search. |
| Apli PMPML | Official PMPML app listing describes digital tickets, live bus tracking, buses, Metro and directions. | PuneSahayak does not sell tickets, receive live bus GPS or provide an official live service feed. | An academic prototype for explaining GTFS data, direct/one-change search and the cost/time assumptions behind a commute comparison. |
| Moovit | Multi-modal public-transport planning, live arrival information where available, service alerts and navigation. | PuneSahayak does not provide equivalent real-time arrivals, alerts, user reports or global network coverage. | Historical Pune camera-count exploration and a local budget tracker are included in one explainable student project. |

## Identified gap and novelty

The project does not claim a new transport network or a better live navigation service. Its practical contribution is combining three explainable student-facing views: (1) estimated transport-mode cost/time comparison, (2) a local PMPML GTFS snapshot journey search with visible source limitations, and (3) personal monthly commute expense tracking. A fourth view exposes historical camera counts without presenting them as live traffic.
